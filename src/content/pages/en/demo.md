Several demonstrations allow you to discover datannur and explore its interface according to different usage contexts.

### Online

Two demos are accessible directly in the browser:

<div class="grid-2">
<div>

**Fictional demo:** to explore the interface in a neutral environment

[Fictional demo](https://dev.datannur.com)

</div>
<div>

**Open data demo:** to see the catalog applied to a concrete case

[Open data demo](https://swiss-demo.datannur.com)

</div>
</div>

### Local

datannur can also run locally or on a shared drive. The fictional demo can be downloaded as a standalone folder, then opened directly in a browser via the index.html file, without installation or an internet connection.

[Download the demo](https://github.com/datannur/datannur/releases/latest/download/datannur-app-latest.zip)
