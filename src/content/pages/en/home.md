## Between Excel and large platforms

Most organizations still do not have a true data catalog. Not because they don't need one, but because existing solutions are too cumbersome, too expensive, or too complex to implement.

Between large governance platforms and overly limited general-purpose tools, there is a lack of a lightweight, open, and sovereign solution.

This is precisely where datannur provides a solution.
