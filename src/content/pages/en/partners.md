datannur is designed first for organizations that produce, publish, or maintain datasets. They can use it directly to inventory their sources, document variables, check quality, and prepare data for publication, BI, or AI.

Partners play a complementary role when clients need help with setup, training, or the structuring of their practices. They can also use datannur in their own audit, data quality, open data preparation, or governance assignments when the tool provides concrete support for field work.

## Why Become a Partner

Many organizations need a simple tool to better understand and document their data. Some can move forward on their own. Others need help defining priorities, clarifying responsibilities, setting good practices, or making usage sustainable over time.

datannur helps support these initiatives with a lightweight, sovereign, open source tool that can be used without heavy infrastructure or license negotiations. The partner keeps their role as advisor, integrator, or trainer. datannur provides the catalog and editor support when usage becomes regular.

## Concrete Use Cases

Use cases are mainly client-side, but they can be enabled or facilitated by a partner:

- inventory and mapping of data sources
- documentation of variables and metadata
- dataset quality assessment
- preparation for open data publication
- scoping of BI or AI projects
- clarification of responsibilities and uses
- GDPR support or data-related compliance

## A Complementary Relationship

The client remains in control of their data and catalog. The partner brings domain expertise when their involvement is useful. datannur ensures product continuity: fixes, updates, technical support, and tool evolution.

This division of roles avoids confusing domain support with editor support. It allows the client to move forward with a tool they control, while benefiting from suitable assistance when the need goes beyond autonomous use. For the partner, datannur can also serve as a working tool to structure a diagnosis, prepare a publication, or keep a clear base when the client wants to continue the work after the engagement.
